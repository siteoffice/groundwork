# The story and the questionnaire

Two pages that open in any browser. Nothing is sent anywhere while you use them.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `groundwork-kit.html` | The story: your company as a development site, folders and plain files as the plans and the record, and agents that build while a person signs every gate. English and Dutch, switch top right. |
| `questionnaire.html` | Ten questions to answer before anything is built, about fifteen minutes. Answers save in your browser. Press Export to get a text file you can send to whoever builds with you. |

BurnMon, the app that shows what your Claude use costs, lives in its own repository.
