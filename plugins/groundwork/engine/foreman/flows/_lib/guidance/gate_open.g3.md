## G3: the learn gate

A sprint has reached a terminal status and the step is ready to be closed out.
G3 opens on what actually happened, not on what was planned.

What to look at: the sprint's own report, the receipts in the flow log, and
whether the result changed anything about the steps still ahead. A sprint that
passed its tests can still have invalidated the next step's premise.

How to answer: `proceed` moves to the next step unchanged, `adapt` moves on
with the remaining steps rewritten in light of what was learned, `close` ends
the flow here because the question has been answered, `park` stops without a
verdict.
