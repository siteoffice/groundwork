# Feedback after two weeks

Thank you for trying Groundwork. Answer in a few lines each, in any form: a reply to the person who
sent you the link, or a new issue. Never paste prompts, keys or customer data.

1. What did you try in the first 15 minutes, and where did you get stuck?
2. Which gate or screen did you use most, and which did you skip?
3. What would you remove?
4. What did you miss that made you go back to your old way of working?
5. Would you use it on your next real project? Why or why not?

Bugs and ideas go in an issue with the matching template, see the README section "Feedback and
issues". Security problems: mail info@zerononsense.dev, not a public issue.
