# Molenwerk BV: instructions

This folder is the company record of Molenwerk BV, a fictional Dutch webshop company used for
testing. One source of truth per fact:

| Question | File |
|---|---|
| What is true now | STATUS.md |
| Hard dates | DEADLINES.md |
| Priority order | 02_roadmap/roadmap.md |
| Why a decision was made | 03_logs/decisions.md |
| What a project is | 01_projects/<project>.md |
| What the company is, who approves | 00_context/brief.md |

Rules: live files state what is true now; durable files get a date prefix; a decision without its
why written down will be taken again. Owner and sole approver: Femke Molenaar.
