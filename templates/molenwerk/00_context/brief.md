# Brief: Molenwerk BV

Molenwerk BV is a fictional Dutch e-commerce company selling refurbished kitchen machines,
founded 2021, seven employees, one warehouse in Zwolle. Owner and sole decision approver:
Femke Molenaar (femke@molenwerk.example). Nothing ships, gets published, or spends money
without her explicit approval. The accountant is external and owns tax filings.
